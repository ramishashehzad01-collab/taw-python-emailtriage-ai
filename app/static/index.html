GOOD TO KNOW

A few simple answers.

Clear boundaries make the workflow easier to trust.

Does EmailTriage send replies for me?

No. It prepares a draft for a person to review. You choose what to edit, save, and eventually send from Gmail.

What does Gmail sync look at?

The project is set up to sync test messages carrying the EmailTriage-Test label. Use fictional or opted-in test messages for demonstrations.

Can I try it without connecting Gmail?

Yes. Open the workspace and use the prefilled sample email to explore classification and reply drafting.
